About BAC

Our Company

Since 1938, BAC has created sustainable comfort cooling, process cooling, and refrigeration solutions for demanding environments around the globe.

History

BAC helped shape evaporative cooling and continues evolving through hybrid, mission-critical, and advanced thermal strategies.

Leadership Focus

Today's BAC story is about balancing performance, water, energy, uptime, and long-term customer support at the same time.

Sustainability

BAC's sustainability story lives across product design, water strategy, energy efficiency, and long-lifecycle equipment decisions.

Who We Serve

HVAC, refrigeration, manufacturing, power and process, and data-center environments with demanding cooling requirements.
